## Search

Press `/` or Control K to search. The index is `search.json`, built from the h2 and h3 sections of every page.

## Page actions

The Page button copies the page as Markdown, or opens it in ChatGPT or Claude. Every page is also published as Markdown, and `llms.txt` lists those copies.
